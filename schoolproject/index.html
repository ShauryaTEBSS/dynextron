Welcome to ABC Library

Welcome to ABC Library, a peaceful learning space for students, teachers, and avid readers. We offer a wide collection of textbooks, fiction, biographies, encyclopedias, and digital study materials.

Facilities Available (Unordered List)
  • Air-conditioned silent reading hall
  • Over 10,000 printed books and magazines
  • Dedicated Audio-Visual learning corner
  • Free Wi-Fi for academic research

Library Rules (Ordered List)
  1. Maintain complete silence inside the reading hall.
  2. A member can borrow a maximum of 2 books for 14 days.
  3. Handle all books and digital equipment with care.
  4. Return borrowed books on time to avoid a late fine of Rs. 5 per day.
