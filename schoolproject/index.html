Welcome to ABC Library

Welcome to ABC Library, your gateway to knowledge, imagination, and academic excellence. Established in 2010, our library houses over 15,000+ books, journals, newspapers, and digital study resources.

Whether you are preparing for Board Examinations, exploring classic literature, or diving into robotics and coding, you will find a quiet and inspiring space here.

Jump to Library Rules ↓


Key Facilities

  • Quiet Reading Hall: Air-conditioned seating for 120 students.
  • Reference Section: NCERT solutions, encyclopedias, and competitive exam guides.
  • Digital Archive: Access to e-books, audiobooks, and educational documentaries.
  • High-Speed Wi-Fi: Free internet access for registered members.

Library Rules & Regulations

  1. Maintain strict silence inside the reading hall at all times.
  2. Each student can issue a maximum of 2 books for 14 days.
  3. A late fine of ₹5 per day is charged after the due date.
  4. Do not tear, fold, or write inside library books.
  5. Always carry your Library Membership Card while borrowing books.
